What’s in the vault

Four drawers. Nothing to remember.

01 / Products & warranties
01

Type the date. The expiry does itself.

Add a product, a purchase date and a duration in months. WarrantyVault works out the expiry and the status, and rejects dates that don’t add up.

purchase_date 2025-01-12 duration_months 24 expiry_date 2027-01-12 ← computed status ACTIVE
02 / Documents
02

The invoice, attached for good.

Upload invoices, warranty cards and receipts. PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB, renamed to safe random filenames and opened only through short-lived signed links.

invoice.pdf → 7f3a…e91.pdf ✓ 4.1 MB card.jpg → c02b…4d7.jpg ✓ 1.2 MB notes.exe → rejected · PDF, JPG, PNG only

How it works

Add. Attach. Forget.

Step one

Add it.

Product, purchase date, duration and coverage notes in one short flow. Dates are validated before anything is saved.

Step two

Attach it.

Snap the invoice or drop the PDF. It’s stored privately and linked to that warranty, ready when you need proof.

Step three

Forget it.

The vault watches the calendar. You hear from it at 30, 15 and 7 days, or whichever thresholds you set in your profile.
